The World's Biggest Clubs

Criteria

Each club gets a tier purely from the titles it has won. There are no votes or subjective scores.

The five tiers

  • GOAT

    Two or more titles across the world title and the top continental competition.

  • Legend

    One title across the world title and the top continental competition.

  • Star

    Two or more titles across the second continental competition, the national league and the national cup.

  • Supporting

    One title in the second continental competition, the national league or the national cup.

  • Traditional

    A traditional top-flight club with no major titles.

How ties are broken

Within each tier, the club with more titles comes first, in this order: world, continental, second-tier continental, national league and national cup.

How to read the data

  • Each continent uses its own competitions. For Saudi clubs, the top continental count adds the AFC Champions League and the Arab Club Champions Cup, at the same level as a Libertadores or a UEFA Champions League, so the ranking measures dominance in each region without comparing the strength of the competitions.
  • "World" covers the club world titles recognised in the project's references, including the former Intercontinental Cup.
  • Clubs listed twice, once for the league and once for the cup, were merged and their tier recalculated.
  • Counts were updated on October 31, 2026 from Wikipedia's lists of winners. Only trophies already recorded in those lists are counted.
