The World's Biggest Clubs

About the project

A ranking of football clubs by what they have actually won, country by country and worldwide.

The World's Biggest Clubs covers clubs from 16 countries and ranks them in five tiers, purely by the titles they have won: world titles, continental competitions, national leagues and cups.

The site also lists the biggest men's national teams by World Cup titles. The debate never ends, which is exactly why the criteria and sources are open for anyone to check.
